## Why this is not a race loss: the restriction, not contention

The observer's snapshots (`walden/observer/2026-10-16/20261009_112609_…`,
9 snapshots from −500ms to +14092ms) show the 09:15 AM row at Northgate
**open through the last snapshot taken (+14092ms)** — nearly 20 seconds
before this request's burst even fired. Nobody else held 09:15 AM in the
window this racer could have reasonably reached it. The three "accepted"
Reserve responses in Race 3's burst corroborate this: the club was still
willing to hand out holds on 09:15 AM at +33.6s.

What actually blocked the booking was the club's own restriction, triggered
because the *same Walden login* (Member B) had already been granted a round
at Northgate that day — Race 2's 09:23 AM, booked under the identical
session 33 seconds earlier. The club enforces this at the final Book Now
step, after already granting (and in this case, triple-granting) a Reserve
hold — the Reserve stage does not check the per-day limit, only the final
submission does. This matches skill §6's "Refused later in the chain... a
club restriction (one round per member per day) surfaces here as a
`Restriction:` dialog" — the first time this report has had cause to cite
that line rather than just footnote it.

### The surplus-hold warning, also worth a note

Three separate burst members (#0, #1, #25) each received an "accepted"
Reserve response for the same 09:15 AM slot, each announced with the
existing `WARNING: Burst member #N was also granted 09:15 AM - a surplus
hold, left to the club's hold timer`. This is the known, already-flagged
behavior from the variable-burst design (skill §7g / `operations/`) — the
club does not reject a second simultaneous hold request, it just grants
both and lets the loser's hold expire. It did not cause today's failure
(the restriction would have blocked the Book Now step regardless of which
hold the code picked), but it is still live and worth remembering if a
future morning's "who held what" story gets confusing.

## Established vs hypothesis

**Established**

- Two of three requests booked at the exact time requested, both on the
  opening burst's first ask (+791ms and +804ms), both confirmed by
  `RESERVATION_CHECK` on the member's own reservations page.
- The third request's failure is a club-side restriction
  ("restricted for 1 round(s) on Northgate per Day"), quoted verbatim from
  the Book Now response, not a timing or contention failure. `RESERVATION_CHECK`
  confirms no reservation exists for it.
- The 09:15 AM fallback slot was open and unheld by anyone else through the
  observer's last snapshot (+14092ms), nearly 20s before this request's own
  burst fired at +33.6s — ruling out "someone else took it" as the cause.
- The 33.6-second delay before Race 3 could even start is architectural: two
  booking requests sharing one Walden login are processed serially, in one
  browser session, and the second cannot begin its own race until the
  first's entire chain (burst, player count, TBD guests, Book Now,
  `RESERVATION_CHECK`) completes.
- Three Reserve attempts (burst #0, #1, #25) were each independently
  "accepted" for the same 09:15 AM slot — the known surplus-hold behavior,
  not a new finding, and not the cause of today's failure.
- All three bookings' confirmation messages delivered (`200 OK`), including
  the failure notice.
- No app-code or terraform change affecting the race path landed between
  the last deploy (`22c9231`, merged 2026-10-04) and this race.

- (Added after merge) `488de6db` and `76a169f4` are one member's duplicate
  request for the same tee time, made on 10-01 and 10-07. The bot accepted
  the second without checking it against the first.
- (Added after merge) The member-facing failure notice led with
  `Reservation Alert: This slot is blocked by another user.`, which the
  booker had already identified as stale (`treating it as stale for the
  rest of the chain`). The restriction followed it. The notice also named
  09:23 AM, the time asked for, not 09:15 AM, the time that failed.

**Hypothesis:** none on the cause of the miss; the restriction message and
`RESERVATION_CHECK` settle it. Why Member B asked twice is not established.

**Cheapest experiment that would discriminate:** none needed.

## Recommendations

1. **No change needed to the race path for Races 1 and 2.** Both resolved
   exactly on the first opening-burst ask, well inside every timing and CPU
   budget.
2. **Refuse the duplicate when it is asked for, not at 06:30.** (Revised
   after merge.) The first version suggested a pre-check in the racer's
   batch. The better place is the request: when a member asks for a date on
   which they already hold a pending Northgate booking, the bot should say so
   then, while it is still a conversation. A batch-time check would only
   move the failure earlier on race morning. Tracked as an issue, together
   with item 3.
3. **Lead the failure notice with the site's actual reason.** (Added after
   merge.) When a `Restriction:` is present, the stale "blocked by another
   user" alert should not precede it, and the notice should name the time
   that was attempted when it differs from the time requested.
4. **Not a fix, just a note:** the surplus-hold behavior (§7g) is still
   live and still worth watching, though it was not the cause of anything
   today.
5. **Tooling nit, not worth a separate investigation:** `fetch_debug_artifacts.py
   fetch` (its `summarize_ledger` step) raised `TypeError: unsupported format
   string passed to NoneType.__format__` while summarizing one ledger after
   downloading it — the download itself completed fine (44 objects), only
   the console summary crashed. Low priority.
