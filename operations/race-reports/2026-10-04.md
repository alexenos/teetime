Added after merge, per skill §7h. Both tasks logged `sending SMS notifications`

```
Telegram API error sending to chat <old group id>: 400 {"ok":false,"error_code":400,
"description":"Bad Request: group chat was upgraded to a supergroup chat",
"parameters":{"migrate_to_chat_id":<new group id>}}
```

`BATCH_JOB: Complete - succeeded=1` and `RACER: task 3/4 finished -
succeeded=1` were logged after the failure regardless. They count bookings, not
deliveries.

**Why only one of the two.** A confirmation goes to the chat the booking was
requested in, saved on the booking when it was made. It does not go to the
configured members group, `TELEGRAM_MEMBERS_CHAT_ID`. The webhook logs show
both requests came from the members' group, under different chat IDs:

- 2026-10-01 07:34 CT: Member B requests Sun 10/11 12:00 PM. The group has
  its original ID, which is saved on their booking.
- Between 2026-10-01 07:37 and 2026-10-02 18:38 CT, Telegram upgrades the
  group to a supergroup, and the group gets a new chat ID.
- 2026-10-02 18:42 CT: Member A requests Sun 10/11 12:08 PM. The group has
  its new ID, which is saved on their booking.

Telegram rejects sends to the old ID. Nothing in the app reads the
`migrate_to_chat_id` it returns or listens for the upgrade event.

**It will recur.** On 2026-10-01, in the group under its old ID, Member B also
requested Fri 10/16 09:23 AM (races 10/09) and Sun 10/18 12:00 PM (races
10/11). If those bookings are still pending, their confirmations will fail the
same way. Requests made from 2026-10-02 on carry the new ID.

## Established vs hypothesis

**Established**

- Both requests were booked at the exact time requested: Member A's 12:08 PM
  granted at +815ms, Member B's 12:00 PM granted at +843ms, both inside the
  opening burst's +815..+1175ms window and close to the +1005ms aim.
- Both final bookings are real reservations, not phrase-match false
  positives — `RESERVATION_CHECK` found each on the member's own reservations
  page, and the postrace sheet independently shows the correct member's name
  on the correct row for each.
- Neither booked slot (nor the two adjacent, unbooked slots the observer also
  tracked) was pre-held: all four read `empty` in the observer before
  flipping within `(+500, +1543]ms`, consistent with — and not narrower
  than — the racer's own `GATE_BRACKET` readings for both slots.
- Both `BATCH_JOB` log lines this morning came from two independent racer-job
  tasks (`RACER: task 1/4` and `task 3/4`), each claiming exactly one
  booking; this is the job-array architecture (skill §1), not a duplicate
  invocation.
- Round trips (2470ms, 3119ms) and burst-timing/CPU diagnostics show no
  timeout risk and no scheduling delay on either task.
- Both bookings logged `agreed=unchecked`, so this morning's ledger row is
  `confirmed_slots: false` even though both requests landed on the exact
  requested time.
- Member A's confirmation was delivered. Member B's was rejected by Telegram
  (400, group upgraded to a supergroup) because their booking stored the group's
  pre-upgrade chat ID. The two bookings were requested before and after the
  upgrade.

**Hypothesis**

- What triggered the upgrade. Telegram upgrades a group automatically on
  certain setting changes, such as granting an admin some rights or making
  history visible. Which change it was is not in these logs.
- That Member B's 10/16 and 10/18 bookings are still pending and carry the old
  ID. The requests are in the webhook logs, but the booking rows were not read,
  because this session has no database access.

**Cheapest experiment that would discriminate:** for the recurrence, a `SELECT`
on `bookings` for the old chat ID (see Recommendations). For the race, none is
needed.
