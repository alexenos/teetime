### The gate-boundary reading for 09:23 AM needs a caveat

The `GATE:` classifier read the grant's club-clock stamp as `:06` and called
it the open second. Per §7a of the skill, that reading is only as good as
the round trip it rode in on: `serverMsPastWindow` is stamped when the club
**answers**, and receipt is only bounded to `[sent, sent+roundTripMs]`. Here
`sent=1026ms`, `roundTripMs=5287ms`, so the true receipt time is bounded
only to `[1026ms, 6313ms]` — the `:06` reading is one point inside a
5.3-second-wide window, not a precise placement of the boundary. What *is*
established, independent of that reading: this was the first (and only
necessary) ask for 09:23 AM, sent right at the sweep's aim point, and it was
granted — no refusal preceded it for this slot. The observer's flip table
(below) narrows the true window further and lands close to the aim, not
near `:06`.

## Established vs hypothesis

**Established**

- 09:23 AM was won cleanly on the first Reserve of its opening burst at
  +1026ms — within 4ms of the sweep's +1030ms aim.
- 08:38 AM was genuinely lost to a different party: every ask (6 burst + 1
  fallback) was refused, and the postrace sheet names a real rival foursome
  (Letbetter, Atencio, Guinn) holding that slot. This is not a pre-held or
  never-bookable slot — the observer shows it `empty` before flipping in the
  same window our own burst ran in.
- The bot correctly fell back and won 08:45 AM at +4856ms.
- Both final bookings are real reservations, not phrase-match false
  positives — `RESERVATION_CHECK` found each by name on the member's own
  reservations page, and the postrace sheet independently shows the correct
  member name on each row.
- Neither booked slot, nor the lost 08:38 AM slot, nor the refused 08:30 AM
  fallback, was pre-held: all four read `empty` in the observer before
  flipping.
- The `GATE:` classifier's `:06` reading for 09:23 AM's grant is unreliable
  on its own (5287ms round trip widens the receipt bound to 5.3s); the
  observer's flip interval narrows this and supports a grant near the aim
  point rather than six seconds later.
- Round trips were within both active timeouts; 5287ms is a new high for a
  race-opening row but left ~4.7s of the 10.0s opening budget unused.
- A postrace-sheet artifact-name collision between the two same-date tasks
  caused one 403 and one skipped sheet capture, with no effect on either
  booking's success and no information actually lost.

**Hypothesis**

- That concurrent same-batch racing (two or more slots) systematically
  raises round trips versus single-slot mornings. 09-19 (3.3–3.6s) and today
  (up to 5.3s) both point this direction but are still only two data points
  at different magnitudes — not yet a trend, per §7b's own standard.

**Cheapest experiment that would discriminate:** none needed to cost a
morning. Continue reading `roundTripMs` on every multi-slot morning; a third
one will either cluster near today's number or 09-19's, or return to
single-slot norms, and that alone settles whether concurrency is the driver.
