## Established vs hypothesis

**Established**

- Both 12:00 PM and 12:08 PM on 2026-10-04 were won inside the opening
  burst, on their planned rungs, and confirmed by `RESERVATION_CHECK`
  against the member's own reservations page for each slot.
- Neither slot was pre-held: the observer shows both reading `empty` at
  parking and flipping to `disabled` only inside the race window.
- Round trips this morning ran 2–5.6s on both tasks, with a new
  overall-morning high of 5649ms, comfortably inside the 10.0s opening
  budget (≥4.3s margin even on the worst row).
- This is the third consecutive multi-slot morning with round trips well
  above the single-slot norm (typically under 1s), and the trend is still
  climbing (3.3–3.6s → 5.3s → 5.6s), not clustering or reverting.
- `BURST_CPU` and `BURST_TIMING` on both tasks show effectively zero
  run-queue delay, zero throttling, and sub-2ms write drift, ruling out our
  own container as the source of the round-trip growth.
- Both racer tasks started ~20.5-20.9s late against the 06:28:00 CT login
  target, with no effect on the outcome (9+ minutes of slack before the
  window).
- No postrace-sheet artifact-name collision recurred: the two racer tasks'
  postrace-sheet uploads landed at different UTC-second object paths
  (`..._113027_for_20261004` and `..._113028_for_20261004`) this time, so
  no 403 and no skipped capture, unlike 2026-09-25.

**Hypothesis**

- That concurrent same-batch slot racing raises round trips against the
  club's server, and that the effect is growing rather than plateauing.
  Three data points now (09-19, 09-25, 09-27), all multi-slot mornings, all
  higher than the last. Still not established as causal — no single-slot
  control morning has been read back-to-back with these for comparison, and
  the mechanism (club-side load vs. something else) is not identified.

**Cheapest experiment that would discriminate:** none that costs a morning.
Keep reading `roundTripMs` on every race, multi-slot and single-slot alike,
and start comparing same-weekday single-slot mornings against these
multi-slot ones directly rather than only tracking the multi-slot number in
isolation - that comparison, not a new booking, is what would show whether
concurrency is really the driver or whether the club's overall load has
just been rising on all mornings.
