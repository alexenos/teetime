## Established vs. hypothesis

**Established, from the logs:**
- The login's submit-button click was intercepted by an element the
  browser identifies as `<div id="ccBanner" class="cc-banner cc-show"
  role="dialog" ...>` — a cookie-consent banner — sitting over the sign-in
  button at the point Selenium tried to click it.
- This happened independently in two separate Chrome sessions three
  minutes apart (observer at 06:25:01 CT, racer at 06:28:08 CT), both on
  `chrome=156.0.8078.79`, both against the same login page and the same
  button id pattern (`_com_liferay_login_web_portlet_LoginPortlet_*`).
- No code change between the last confirmed clean login (2026-11-06) and
  this morning touched the login path in either file.
- `SLOT_AGREEMENT` logged `agreed=sheet` for the one request this morning,
  so the member's requested time was a tee time the sheet actually offered
  as of the last read — this is about slot agreement, not about whether the
  booking succeeded, and it did not.
- The failure notice to the member sent and was acknowledged `200 OK`.

**Not established / hypothesis:**
- Whether `ccBanner` is a new, permanent fixture of Walden's login page or
  something shown intermittently (e.g., once per session, first-visit-only,
  or geolocation/consent-tracking dependent). This is the first appearance
  of this banner anywhere in this repository's logs or reports. No evidence
  here says which.
- Whether the banner would have gone away on its own (a longer wait before
  clicking) or whether it reliably sits on top of the submit button and
  needs to be dismissed or bypassed every time. Nothing in the two observed
  failures distinguishes these.
- Why it was not seen before this morning — a site-side change in the last
  few days is the natural guess given a 2-month run of clean logins, but
  that is not confirmed by anything here.

## Recommendations

**A likely fix, offered as a first-pass guess rather than a diagnosis:**
the login flow's submit click is a plain Selenium `.click()`
(`app/providers/walden_provider.py:933`, mirrored at
`app/observer/sheet.py:176`), which fails outright when another element
overlaps the target. Elsewhere in `walden_provider.py` an obscured or
not-yet-interactable element is clicked via
`driver.execute_script("arguments[0].click();", element)` instead (e.g.
lines 2268, 2310, 2591, 5811, 5928, and the observer's own line 438) — the
same pattern would sidestep a banner sitting on top of the button, if the
banner is harmless to skip past. Alternatively, if the banner carries a
dismiss/accept control, clicking that first would be the more conservative
fix. Both are guesses with no investigation behind them beyond reading the
one error message; this may be worth a deeper investigation pass (a
stronger model than this session runs by default) before changing the
login path, particularly to see whether the banner reproduces on a plain
page load outside of race conditions.
